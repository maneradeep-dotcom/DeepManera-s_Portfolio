SITE INFORMATION

Privacy, in plain language.

This draft describes the behavior implemented in this static portfolio. It is not legal advice or a statement about another product or client system. Review the owner, hosting provider and retention details before using this policy on a public business site.

Site operator
Deep Manera
Thane, Maharashtra, India

Contact
scriptinkvisualizer@gmail.com

Policy basis
Static portfolio and email-based enquiries. Update if the implementation changes.

1. What this site does

The portfolio serves static pages, styles, scripts, a public package-estimate JSON file and an original SVG monogram, wordmark and favicon. It has no sign-in, user account, database, advertising pixels, third-party font embeds, chat widget, analytics script or server-side contact endpoint. Project analytics are disabled in this Manus project configuration. The light/dark display control is temporary and does not save a cookie or browser-storage preference.

The quote calculator runs in your browser. Package options and any details you type remain in that page unless you choose an action. Choosing “Prepare email enquiry” opens your own email application with a message draft; the site does not submit that content to a server or know whether you send it.

2. Data that may be processed

If you choose to send an email, your email provider and the recipient’s email provider process the address, message and any information you include so the message can be delivered. The website owner may then read and reply to it in their email account. Do not include passwords, payment data, government identifiers, customer lists, employee records or confidential production information in an initial enquiry.

The hosting, edge or delivery provider may process connection and request metadata, such as IP address, requested page and security logs, to deliver and protect the site. The provider, data region and log-retention period depend on where the owner deploys this package and have not been established by this source bundle. Review the selected host's current privacy and logging terms before publication.

3. Cookies, analytics and external services

This site's source does not set cookies or run analytics, advertising, social tracking or embedded third-party media. The platform site-analytics setting for the Manus project was turned off; platform, host or CDN operational logs may still exist independently. If this site is later moved to Vercel or another host, that provider's own processing and any optional features must be checked separately.

Email links use the mailto: scheme. Opening one hands control to your device's configured mail application or email service. Its own privacy terms apply. The project includes no payment service, mailing list, SMS, map, third-party API or external asset dependency.

4. Purpose, access and retention

Any voluntarily emailed information is used to understand and respond to the enquiry. This static site has no server-side form inbox and no CRM connection. The owner should restrict access to the email account, avoid retaining information longer than needed, and delete or anonymise messages when appropriate under the applicable agreement and law. Email copies, provider backups and delivery logs are controlled by the relevant email providers; this site cannot erase those copies.

No visitor profile, use history or quote submission is stored by the website. An estimate is produced locally for convenience and is not sent or saved unless you make a separate choice in your email client.

5. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose not to open or send an email and can close or clear the calculator page. For a question about information you have voluntarily emailed to Deep, use this privacy-request link. The owner will review the request under the laws that apply; no legal entitlement, timing or outcome is guaranteed by this draft.

6. Children, changes and review

This portfolio is intended for people considering a professional project; it is not designed to collect information from children. If the owner becomes aware that a child's personal information has been sent, they should seek qualified advice and handle it appropriately.

This policy should be updated whenever a form, tracker, embedded service, account, CRM or storage provider is added or the hosting arrangement changes. There is no effective-date claim here because the live hosting configuration has not been confirmed.

7. India-specific legal review

Before public commercial use, ask a qualified Indian privacy professional to assess whether and how India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the DPDP Rules, 2025, their commencement provisions, and other applicable laws apply to the actual operator, visitors, hosting and email arrangement. Applicable requirements can depend on current commencement and context; this page does not assert compliance or offer legal advice.

Owner action before launch: confirm the site operator/contact, hosting and email providers, available log/retention practices, retention for enquiry email, privacy-request handling and jurisdiction-specific notices. This document is a product-specific draft, not a legally reviewed policy.
