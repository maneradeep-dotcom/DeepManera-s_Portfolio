Scroll, story & interaction

Motion should make sense.

This page turns the project path into a readable sequence, then lets you try two small workflow examples. Motion is restrained, user-controlled by scrolling and removed when reduced motion is preferred.

The interactive examples use invented labels and sample records. They are explanations, not screenshots or previews of a live client system.

SCROLL NARRATIVE / 01–04

Four chapters. One clear route.

Use the chapter links, scroll normally, or skip ahead. There is no pinned scroll, forced timing or automatic audio. Each panel arrives gently and remains available at your own pace.

FIELD NOTES / 01

Find the real friction.

Who does the work? Where does the handoff fail? What needs to be recorded or approved?

FIELD NOTES / 02

Turn it into a clear agreement.

Agree workflows, exclusions, dependencies, cost, review points, dates and how “done” will be checked.

FIELD NOTES / 03

Build, then look together.

Share reviewable milestones. Check the path through the system and handle changes in writing.

FIELD NOTES / 04

Leave a useful handover.

Review agreed paths, document setup and transfer only the deliverables listed in the approved scope.

The four-step storytelling here is original code. It is not a captured or re-used image from any archive.
